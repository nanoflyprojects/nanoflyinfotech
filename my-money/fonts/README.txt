Brand fonts (second brand kit)
- Posterama: headlines.  Sentic: section titles.  Body: Red Hat Display (loaded from Google Fonts).

Posterama and Sentic are licensed fonts and are not on Google Fonts, so they are not included.
If you own them, convert to WOFF2 and save as:
  fonts/Posterama.woff2
  fonts/Sentic.woff2
The site picks them up automatically (css/style.css). Until then Red Hat Display is used everywhere.
