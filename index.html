What does NanoFLY InfoTech do?

NanoFLY InfoTech is a digital marketing and software company in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. We offer social media management, SEO and paid ads, website development, custom software and digital marketing training.

How much do your social media management plans cost?

Monthly plans start at ₹6,999 for account maintenance. The Basic plan is ₹11,999, Silver is ₹21,999, Gold is ₹34,999 and Platinum is ₹49,999 per month. All prices are flat and in rupees.

How much does a website cost?

A 5-page portfolio website is ₹14,999 one-time and an e-commerce website with 50 products is ₹24,999 one-time. Both include a one-year domain in your business name, SEO optimization, a Google Business account and one year of maintenance.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. The custom software launch plan is ₹30,000 and includes customized software development, one year of maintenance, hosting, a database, mail integration and a domain email address.

How can I verify an internship certificate?

Every intern receives a certificate with its own verification page. Search the name on our Certifications page to confirm it.
