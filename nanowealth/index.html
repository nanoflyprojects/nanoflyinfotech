Questions, answered

Where is my data stored?

In a Google Sheet that Nano Wealth creates in your own Google Drive the first time you sign in. You are the owner. Nano Wealth’s servers don’t keep a copy.

Can the admin see my transactions?

No. The admin portal only shows account details such as name, email, join date and status. It has no connection to your sheet and cannot open it.

What permission does Nano Wealth ask Google for?

Only drive.file — access limited to files the app itself created. It cannot browse the rest of your Drive.

What if I stop using Nano Wealth?

Your sheet stays in your Drive and keeps working as a normal spreadsheet. You can delete your account from Settings at any time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — Nano Wealth is fully responsive and can be installed as an app from your browser.
